## Jeepney fares

The minimum fare for a traditional jeepney is ₱13 for the first 4 kilometers, plus about ₱1.80 for every
additional kilometer. Modern (e-)jeepneys have a minimum fare of about ₱15.
Students, senior citizens, and PWDs get a 20% fare discount when they show a valid ID.
A daily round trip of 8 to 10 km by jeep usually costs ₱26 to ₱40.

## Bus and UV Express

City bus fares start at about ₱13 to ₱15 for ordinary buses and higher for air-conditioned buses, depending on distance.
UV Express fares are distance-based and usually cost more than jeepneys for the same route.

## MRT and LRT

Train fares are distance-based. MRT-3 fares range from about ₱13 to ₱28 per trip.
LRT-1 and LRT-2 fares range from about ₱15 to ₱35 per trip depending on the stations.
Using a beep card is faster but costs the same per trip.

## Ride-hailing and tricycles

Grab, Joyride, and Angkas cost much more than public transport, especially during rush hour or rain (surge pricing).
Tricycle fares are set by each city or municipality, so they vary by place.

## Ways to lower transport spending

Plan a fixed daily pamasahe budget and keep it in a separate wallet.
Use the student discount every time. Combine errands into one trip.
Leave earlier to avoid needing a Grab when running late.
