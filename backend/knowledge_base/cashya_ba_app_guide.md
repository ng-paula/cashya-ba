# Cashya Ba? App Guide

## How the daily allowance is computed

Cashya Ba? subtracts fixed expenses (like rent and bills) from your total baon or sahod to get the money for daily spending.
It divides that by the number of days left, including today, to get today's allowance.
The allowance is recomputed every day: if you spend less today, tomorrow's allowance goes up; if you overspend, it goes down.

## What the status means

"Cashya pa" means you are on track. "Medyo tight" means today's allowance is below your original plan.
"Hindi cashya" means you already went over today's allowance or, at your average pace, the money will run out before the period ends.

## Run-out forecast

The run-out forecast divides your spendable budget by your average daily spending so far.
If the result is fewer days than your budget period, the app warns you which day the money will run out.

## Alerts and automation

The app can send a morning message with today's allowance, warnings at 50%, 80%, and 100% of the budget,
a warning when the forecast says the money won't last, an evening reminder if nothing was logged, and a weekly report.
Alerts are sent through Telegram or email and can be turned on or off in Settings.

## Household mode

A parent account can link a child account, set the child's baon, and see a spending summary.
A child account can log expenses but cannot create or change its own budget.

## Privacy

Card numbers, mobile numbers, and email addresses typed in the chat are masked before they reach the AI or the database.
The assistant does not give investment, loan, or gambling advice.
