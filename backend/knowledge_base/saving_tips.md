# Saving Tips for Students and Workers

> Sample knowledge-base document for the Cashya Ba? prototype.

## Emergency fund

An emergency fund is money kept only for real emergencies like illness, job loss, or urgent repairs.
Start with a goal of one month of expenses, then build it up to three to six months.
Keep it in a separate savings account or e-wallet that you don't use for daily spending.

## Tips for students on baon

Ask for baon weekly instead of daily so you learn to plan for several days.
Set aside a small amount (₱10 to ₱20) every day before spending; it adds up to ₱300 to ₱600 a month.
Bring water and snacks from home. Share printing and project costs with groupmates.
Use the student discount on jeeps, buses, and trains.

## Tips for workers on sahod (15th and 30th)

Pay yourself first: move savings to a separate account on payday.
Set aside rent and bills as fixed expenses on the same day.
Watch out for "payday spending" in the first three days after sahod; this is when most overspending happens.
Plan big purchases as savings goals instead of buying on impulse.

## Avoid debt traps

Avoid borrowing from online lending apps with very high interest or harassment practices.
Avoid "5-6" informal lending, where you pay back 20% more within a short time.
Gambling, including online sabong and casino apps, is not a way to grow money and often leads to losses.
If you are already in debt, list all debts and pay the one with the highest interest first.

## Buy now, pay later

Installments and "pay later" options make items feel cheaper but lock future income.
Only use them for needs, and include the monthly payment as a fixed expense in your budget.
