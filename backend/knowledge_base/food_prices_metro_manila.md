# Food Prices and Daily Food Budget — Metro Manila

## Typical daily food budget

For one person in Metro Manila, a modest food budget is about ₱200 to ₱300 per day when eating mostly
at carinderias and cooking sometimes. Eating at fast-food chains for every meal usually costs ₱350 to ₱500 per day.
Students who bring baon from home can often keep food spending under ₱150 per day.

## Carinderia and fast-food meal prices

A carinderia meal of rice plus one ulam usually costs ₱70 to ₱120.
A silog meal (tapsilog, longsilog) costs about ₱80 to ₱130.
A fast-food value meal with rice and drink costs about ₱90 to ₱180.
Milk tea costs about ₱90 to ₱180 per cup, which adds up quickly if bought daily.

## Basic grocery items (approximate)

Regular or well-milled rice: about ₱45 to ₱55 per kilo.
Eggs: about ₱7 to ₱10 each.
Instant noodles: about ₱10 to ₱18 per pack.
Canned sardines (155g): about ₱22 to ₱30.
Pandesal: about ₱3 to ₱6 per piece.

## Ways to lower food spending

Cook rice and ulam in batches for 2 to 3 days. Bring baon to school or work at least twice a week.
Buy fruits and vegetables in palengke near closing time for lower prices.
Limit milk tea and coffee shop drinks to once a week; replacing 4 cups a month at ₱120 saves about ₱480.
