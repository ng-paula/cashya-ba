## Electricity (Meralco)

The Meralco residential rate has been roughly ₱11 to ₱13 per kilowatt-hour (kWh) in recent years and changes every month.
A small household using 150 to 200 kWh a month typically pays about ₱1,700 to ₱2,600.
An electric fan uses about 50 to 75 watts; an aircon unit uses 600 to 1,500 watts, so aircon use is the biggest driver of high bills.

## Water

Monthly water bills for a small household in Metro Manila are commonly ₱300 to ₱800, depending on usage and provider.

## Rent and bedspace

Bedspace near universities and business districts in Metro Manila commonly costs ₱2,500 to ₱5,000 per month.
A studio unit commonly costs ₱8,000 to ₱15,000 per month. Rent should ideally take no more than 30% of income.

## Mobile load and data

Data promos commonly cost ₱50 to ₱99 for a few days and ₱299 to ₱599 for 30 days.
A monthly promo is usually cheaper per day than buying small promos repeatedly.
Postpaid plans make sense only if the monthly fee is lower than what you already spend on prepaid load.

## Treat bills as fixed expenses

Rent, electricity, water, and monthly load should be set aside first as fixed expenses,
so the daily allowance only covers money that is truly free to spend.
